Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: February 22, 2026

1. 14-Day Returns

We want you to be 100% satisfied with your purchase. You can return any item within 14 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange, provided it is in its original, unused condition.

2. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive an item that is damaged or incorrect, please CONTACT US within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange for a replacement or a full refund at no additional cost to you.

3. Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will process your refund. The funds will be returned to your original payment method (bank account or card) via PayFast within 5-7 business days.

4. Exclusions

Due to hygiene reasons, certain items like swimwear, jewelry, and opened personal care products cannot be returned unless they are defective.

5. How to Start a Return

To start a return, please visit your Order History in your profile or email us at adamdono100@gmail.com with your order number.
